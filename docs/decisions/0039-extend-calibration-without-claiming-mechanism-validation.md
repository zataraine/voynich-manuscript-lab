Date: 2026-10-14
Status: accepted

and heterogeneous supplied boundaries. Freeze the largest primary length before

target access is authorized. Passing is only evidence for this tested domain.

statistical replication and estimator calibration. An upstream run under a new
recorded seed cannot claim byte-for-byte reproduction of unseeded published data.
