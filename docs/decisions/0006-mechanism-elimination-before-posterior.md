# Decision 0006: eliminate explicit mechanisms before estimating a posterior

- Date: 2026-08-30
- Status: accepted

## Context

The research question asks for the probability that Voynichese is a manufactured
language rather than entirely nonsensical hoax text. Regularity alone cannot
separate these classes because a nonsemantic generator can be deliberately
structured, while an encoded payload can suppress familiar linguistic signals.
The lab has one target book and does not yet have a balanced, independently
calibrated population of meaningful and nonsemantic generator families.

## Decision

The first lane will report held-out compatibility with named mechanisms, effect
sizes, empirical null distributions, and corrected p-values. It will explicitly
leave posterior probability unset. Physical pages are the split unit. Local
language models retrieve methods and review immutable numeric results but never
calculate target statistics or semantically embed manuscript text.

A posterior lane may be added only after positive controls, negative controls,
leave-one-generator-family-out validation, explicit priors, and sensitivity
analysis exist.

## Consequences

The first result may reject simplistic randomness or specific pseudo-text
families but cannot establish meaning. This is less rhetorically satisfying than
a percentage, but it is falsifiable, extensible, and makes later odds auditable.
The copy/mutate comparator is retained even if it fits well, because a strong
nonsemantic control is more informative than an easily defeated shuffle.
