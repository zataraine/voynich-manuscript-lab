# ADR 0021: Calibrate the replicated signature on external mechanisms

## Status

Accepted on 2026-09-01.

## Context

E-012 shows that eight simple-null rejections survive transcription choice, but
copy/mutate pseudo-text remains compatible on two underlying measurements.
Non-randomness is not specific to an intentional payload, and repeated target
analysis would invite overfitting.

## Decision

Exclude Voynichese from E-013. Freeze the eight E-012 effects and test them with
whole-document grouping against natural, ciphered, procedurally generated,
human-gibberish, multilingual/conlang, and Naibbe controls. Require both omitted-
mechanism development performance and independent-source performance. Treat
human gibberish and Naibbe as external gates, not anecdotes.

## Consequences

- The experiment can falsify the signature without spending another target
  degree of freedom.
- A family-specific shortcut is exposed by complete-family holdout.
- Failure closes this low-level signature branch rather than prompting target-
  tuned thresholds.
- Passing permits only a new preregistration for target application, not a hoax
  probability or decipherment claim.
