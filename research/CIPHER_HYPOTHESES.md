# Cipher hypothesis ledger

Do not add a candidate plaintext alone. For every entry record date, source and
numeric-artifact hashes, unitization, ordered cipher stages, parameter/search
space, score chosen before search, training/held-out pages, null families,
multiple-testing correction, seeds, output path, and rejection conditions.

No manuscript-specific cipher hypothesis has been registered yet. E-003 is a
control-domain transfer campaign over known synthetic mechanisms; it is not a
manuscript cipher hypothesis and creates no candidate plaintext.
